# Small evidence packet

Research question: does retrieval-time query expansion improve rare-entity recall without degrading common-entity precision?

- Study A (full paper, internal benchmark, n=3 seeds): reports +6.1 points rare-entity recall and -0.4 points common-entity precision. The benchmark and code are available, but only one language is tested.
- Study B (abstract only): reports no rare-entity recall improvement. Dataset, seed count, and expansion policy are not described.
- Study C (workshop abstract): reports +4 points rare-entity recall, but uses the same benchmark and authors as Study A; independence is unclear.
- Study D (full paper, different domain, n=5 seeds): reports better recall only when entity descriptions exceed 20 tokens, with a 2-point precision loss on short descriptions.

No systematic search or field-wide coverage assessment has been performed. Produce research candidates only; selection and experiment design are out of scope.
